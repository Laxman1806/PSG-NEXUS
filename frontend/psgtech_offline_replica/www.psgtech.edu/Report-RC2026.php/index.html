Research Conclave 2026

Date: May 14 - 15, 2026

Organised by: PSG College of Technology

The Fifth National Level Research Conclave 2026 (RC2026), organized by PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore, was held on May 14 and 15, 2026, with the theme, “Advancing Societally Relevant Applications of Knowledge through Scientific Research.” The conclave was conceptualized to provide a dynamic platform for interdisciplinary collaboration, innovation, and dissemination of cutting-edge research across science and engineering.

This vibrant event brought together over 350 participants, including postgraduate students, PhD scholars, undergraduate students, faculty members, and professionals from industry. Registration, paper submissions, and the peer-review process were efficiently managed through the official website:https://researchconclave.psgtech.ac.in/

The event was convened under the leadership of:

  • Dr. P. R. Thyla, Dean (Research), and
  • Dr. J. Krishnamoorthi, Associate Dean (Research) ( HoD, Dept of Metallurgical Engg)
The team of organising secretaries included:
  • Dr. B. Vinothkumar, Professor, Dept of Information Technology
  • Dr. M. Bagyalakshmi, Assistant Professor, Dept of Mathematics
  • Dr.P.Sivakumar, Assistant Professor, Dept of EEE.
  • Dr. D. Dhanalakshmi, Assistant Professor, Dept of Applied Science
  • Dr. M. Kalaiarasan, Assistant Professor, Dept of Mechanical Engineering.
  • Dr.R.Mohanraj , Assistant Professor, Dept of Production Engineering.

The conclave was formally inaugurated on May 14, 2026, with Dr.Vaiyanathan Subramanian, Deputy Director, Globel foundries, Bengaluru as the Chief Guest. Dr. K. Prakasan, Principal, PSG College of Technology, presided over the ceremony.

The event featured four thought-provoking keynote sessions delivered by eminent experts:

  1. Dr.Vaiyanathan Subramanian on “ Research , Innovation and beyond”
  2. Dr.SurendranCherukodan from Central Library, Cochin University of Science and Arts on “academic publishing: platforms, metrics and visibility”.
  3. Dr.Sudhasree Nag from Clarivate Analytics, Mumbai on “ Building Global reputation and Research impact through wed of Science”
  4. Dr.Ankineedu Velaga on “Idea factories that can generate great products”

All submitted research papers underwent a rigorous peer-review process by internal and external reviewers. After evaluation and revisions, 339 papers were selected for presentation. These were organized into 51 parallel contributory sessions across 12 departments, spanning the following eight major thematic domains:

  • Advances in Biomaterials and Biotechnology
  • Advancements in Electronics and Intelligent Systems
  • Automation and Digital transformation
  • Humanities and Entrepreneurship
  • Physical and mathematical sciences
  • Sustaianable and Smart Manufacturing
  • Reinforcements in Civil Engg and Textile Technologyp
  • Transformation innovatives in computing

The conclave concluded with valedictory function on 15th May 2026. The best presentations under each domain were awarded with cash prizes in the presence of sponsors. The certificates were also distributed to all the participants. Select papers presented in the conclave will be brought out as proceedings volume by Taylor & Francis, with an ISBN, individual DOIs for each paper.
