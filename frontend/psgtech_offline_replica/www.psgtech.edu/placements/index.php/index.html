PROFILE

PSG College of Technology has a dedicated and fully independent Career Development Centre that actively facilitates campus recruitment for both multinational and national organizations. The office is headed by a Dean of Placement & Training, supported by a Corporate Liaison Officer, a Lead - Placement, and a Placement Officer. The Lead - Placement, manages recruitment drives and overall operations, serving as the Single Point of Contact (SPoC) for all recruiting companies. Individual Coordinators oversee internship and training activities related to placement.Additionally, a team of enthusiastic student placement representatives,guided by the faculty placement coordinatorsfrom various programmes, assist in the smooth functioning of placement activities.

The Career Development Centre operates from a modern, air-conditioned facility equipped with state-of-the-art infrastructure for conducting Pre-Placement Talks, Aptitude Tests, Written Tests, Group Discussions, and Interviews. With a network of over 400 computer systems, the office seamlessly handles computer-based aptitude and coding assessments. Over the years, it has continually enhanced its capabilities, playing a vital role in guiding and preparing students for successful careers.

Each year, more than 400 renowned national and multinational companies visit the campus for recruitment, reflecting the strong industry connect and trust PSG College of Technology has built over time.

Recognized as one of the top fifteen technical institutes in India in terms of campus placements, PSG College of Technology consistently witnesses a large number of placement offers from leading organizations across the country.

Vision

The Career Development Centre at PSG College of Technology envisions empowering every student to become a future-ready professional with exceptional skills, strong ethical values, and social responsibility, by providing comprehensive career guidance, fostering robust industry connections, and nurturing a recruitment-focused ecosystem that supports fulfilling careers and advanced studies for the benefit of both our students and employers.

Mission

To foster long-term, meaningful partnerships with industry by:

  • Streamlining the talent acquisition process for internships and full-time employment opportunities
  • Integrating industry expertise into the academic value chain to ensure continued relevance, and
  • Leveraging the global network of senior alumni in industry and academia to strengthen academic processes and promote lifelong learning.

Dean's Desk

Greetings from the Career Development Centreof PSG College of Technology!

Thank you for your interest in our institution. At PSG College of Technology, our Career Development Centre is dedicated to providing an exceptional environment for companies to conduct recruitment drives smoothly, efficiently, and professionally. We facilitate both on-campus and virtual recruitment drives, ensuring the best possible opportunities for every graduating student.

Our Career Development Centreis equipped with state-of-the-art facilities to seamlessly conduct all placement-related activities, including Pre-Placement Talks, Written/Online Tests, Group Discussions, and Interviews. Beyond placements, we actively counsel and guide our students in career planning and preparation, ensuring they are well-equipped for successful professional journeys.

Shaped by the holistic vision of our institution, our students are well-rounded, industry-ready professionals, excelling in both technical expertise and soft skills. As an autonomous institution, we deliver a dynamic and rigorous academic experience, enabling our students to master both fundamental principles and the latest advancements in their respective fields.

Each year, over 400 reputed national and multinational companies visit our campus for recruitment, reflecting the strong industry partnerships we have cultivated. PSG College of Technology consistently maintains an impressive placement record, a testament to the quality of our students and the trust placed in us by the industry.

Nationally, PSG College of Technology ranks among the top fifteen technical institutes in terms of placement numbers, with the Ministry of Education ranking us 67th among all engineering institutions in India.

We welcome you to explore the information on our placement process(both full time and internship), curriculum of various academic programmes, departments, institution , and accreditation achievements.
