What We do ?

We hold a multitude of activities in a variety of fields to engage students from the Circuit Branches and Computing departments. We strongly strive to expose students to competitive environments, thereby supporting them in the development of technical and non-technical skills.

SRiSHTi

SRiSHTi, hosted by the IEEE SC 12951, is an Inter-Collegiate technical symposium comprising several events, workshops, hackathons Flagship events and many more.

Atom

ATOM is an Intra-Collegiate symposium, where the Circuit branches of PSG College of Technology coalesce and host a variety of different events.

Inauguration

The Inaugural event marks the start of the next operational year for the IEEE Students Chapter 12951.

Valedictory

The Valedictory event marks the end of a productive and fulfilling year for the IEEE Students Chapter 12951.
