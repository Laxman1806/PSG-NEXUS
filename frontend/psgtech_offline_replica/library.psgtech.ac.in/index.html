About Dr. GRD Memorial Library

Our Dr. GRD Memorial Library has rich collection of books, journals and e-resources catering to students, research scholars and faculty members. Presently, over 2.60 Lakhs books, 13053 CDs/DVDs and 195 printed journals are possessed by the library access to more than 1078 online electronic journals and 941 online DELNET journals. The library is automated having an online portal that can be easily accessed by the users. The library implemented KOHA which is an integrated Library Management System that supports all housekeeping operations of the library. There is a separate Digital Library having 122 computers running on high speed internet. The library system and staff is meticulously focused for providing comfortable, user friendly environment so as to enables learning and knowledge creation.

Facilities and Services

"Find out the facilities and services available for Library Users".

Digital Library

Computers in the Library are connected to the internet and have specific resources to aid your research. Black-and-white and colour printers are available. WiFi is free to anyone with a laptop or mobile device.

Inter-library Loan

We supply books and copies of books to other institutions, through the inter-library loans by DELNET.

Digital Learning Center

We provide facilities to conduct sessions, workshops, webinars and video conferencing system to the faculty and research scholars.

Institutional Repository

We archived all the in-house publications like articles, theses, project reports and club activities.

Research Support

The Library offers a range of support services for the research activities of the faculty, fellows and students of PSG Tech. We provide services, resources, and expertise to support every phase of the research - from discovery and design to creation, strategic positioning and sharing.

Remote Access

Remote access to library resources such as E-Books, E-Journals and E-Databases.
