Message from the Managing Trustee

Ever since the wheel was invented, mankind has undergone phenomenal changes. Technology has been vibrant. Along the path, its constant urge for making himself comfortable was realized with every new invention. It has now reached a stage where humans face uncontrolled changes. Technology has become indispensable, affecting everyone and changing the very way of life and society.

PSG College of Technology, a higher education institution that strives to make the mind, body and soul of every student complete, uses an integrated approach for human development. The institution has produced leaders and pioneers in engineering and technology, contributing to the development of industry and society.
